Grovekeeper

Plan your day. Grow your Grove.

Grovekeeper is a desktop planner for Windows. Your tasks and daily routines live beside a small fantasy Grove with a familiar companion, and the Grove grows and changes as you finish real things in your life.

What it does

  • Today and This week: plan tasks by day, keep simple daily routines, and check them off.
  • A Grove that grows: completing tasks earns levels and cosmetic rewards for your Grove and companion.
  • Always-on-top checklist: a small overlay keeps today's list beside your work.
  • Calendar view: a month view that can show holidays, calendar files you import, and, if you choose, your Google Calendar events.

Google Calendar (optional)

If you connect your Google account, Grovekeeper reads your calendar list and events so it can show them in its Month view. It asks only for read-only access: it never creates, changes or deletes anything in your Google account. Your events stay on your own computer and are never sent anywhere else. You can disconnect at any time.

Read the full privacy policy.

Private by design

Grovekeeper has no accounts, no cloud storage, no ads and no tracking. Everything you plan is stored on your own computer.
